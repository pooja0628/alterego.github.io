I went to watch a movie today - 12th Fail. It's a story of IPS office Manoj Kumar Sharma, who fought against all odds in his life, negated the voices which were pulling him back and achieved what he aspired to be. Its an excellent movie, a must watch for hustlers so that they can gather the courage to dream bigger and a must must watch for the privileged (like me) who start thinking they are going through a lot to achieve their goals and in result, they downsize their dreams.

I am here to write my takeaway from Manoj's life. Before that, I want to remember one distant similarity Manoj and I have - about 'No cheating' policy. Manoj's school had an 'arrangement' so that everyone can pass the exam. The teachers helped children by allowing them to cheat. Obviously, it didnt help the students. But one odd day, when an officer in Manoj's village decided that he wouldn't let students cheat, everybody in school failed. Manoj got inspired by this police officer and asked him how he could become an officer like him and he got a suggestion that he must not cheat to move ahead in life. Manoj made it the motto of his life. A small incident changed course of action for one child. Similarly, I had a senior in school. Her name is Neha. Perhaps we discussed or someone told me about her that she never cheated in exams, like never. She was kind of inspiration to me already and I was also in dilemma about exam cheating. My heart said no cheating but I was doing it because of peer pressure. But after knowing about Neha Didi, I decided on that very moment that I would never ever cheat again in exam and I followed it throughout my life and it worked in my favour always. A simple influence and decision changed my thought process to such huge level. So Manoj and I, we both dont cheat.

About film's takeaways. The few things I notice in every success story.

Inspiration

Courage

Simple and clear dream

Structured preparation

Support

Failure and improvement

Performance

Confidence

Manoj gets inspired by a police officer in his village. That gave birth to his aspirations. He has a dream. Not very complex with plan A , plan B, plan C, Just once simple clear dream, no distractions. He identifies the first step towards that and starts preparing. He has lot of people telling him that he wont be able to do it but he just needed one Shraddha, one Gauri Bhaiya and one Pandey to help him stick to his plan. No success story is complete without a person who showed the belief in you when nobody else did. Gives you the motivation to surround yourself with right people or just be that person for someone else. Both ways you will gain an extraordinary satisfaction of doing something right. Manoj can hold his courage just because these friends support him in tough times - financially, emotionally, logistically etc. Shraddha is Manoj's love interest and she is there with him always. Manoj is not intimidated by the success Shraddha is gaining, nor is Shraddha discouraged by failures Manoj is facing. Behind every successful man, there is a woman? Probably. But I want to say it differently. Behind every aspirational person, there is a partner who is attracted to the light in eyes of the other person who talks about their aspiration. So it's the courage and willingness to achieve great heights that attract people. Give them that and they will turn the world to support you. Manoj failed for different reasons in his preparation but he has narrowed his focus on one clear dream so much that he improves himself continuously with no boundaries. He doesnt have a choice to give up. He prepares and delivers, learns and fails, re-learn and re-start. So much effort to achieve one goal. Its not a blind preparation towards a hypothetical dream. Its very structured. At the end, its his preparation that is giving him confidence , not the result of the exam. Result of the exam is dependent on his confidence. A full circle.

Concluding, the movie was great and affected me in so many ways. I am attracted to simplicity again. How the small things should be preserved and cultivated. The film taught me what is my duty as a partner. My job can't be just to enjoy the success of my family, my job is travel the path to success with them and accept them as they are even if they cant reach to the other side of the path. Just hold hands.
